# Principles corpus starter

This starter file is intentionally a small seed for local RAGFlow setup. Replace it with licensed or user-owned primary sources before using the app for serious research.

## Evidence policy

Principles separates the graph from evidence. A graph node is a hypothesis, relationship, or lens. It becomes evidence only when a retrieval system returns a source chunk with provenance.

## Council protocol

The council should distinguish observation, interpretation, assumption, and recommendation. It should state when evidence is insufficient, preserve disagreements between thinkers, and never fabricate a citation.

## Thinker lenses

Ray Dalio contributes systems thinking, reality orientation, incentives, and reflective learning.

Charlie Munger contributes inversion, mental models, incentive analysis, and avoidance of predictable error.

Warren Buffett contributes long-term compounding, integrity, circle of competence, and margin of safety.

Karl Marx contributes analysis of material conditions, power, class interests, and institutional contradiction.

Hồ Chí Minh contributes practice, people-centered organization, learning, and adaptation to concrete conditions.

Marcus Aurelius contributes agency, judgment, duty, and steadiness under uncertainty.
